We welcome inquiries from students and researchers interested in computer vision, machine learning, and related areas. If your interests align with our work, please feel free to contact us by email.

Please briefly describe your academic or research background, the questions that interest you, and how you would like to participate. Include a CV or other relevant materials. Prospective students should indicate the program and intended start date; potential collaborators may outline the topic they would like to explore together.

We respond based on current research plans, supervision capacity, and alignment of interests. You are also welcome to stay in touch and follow future updates.
